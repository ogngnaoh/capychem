CapyChem CDK2 science components and reference data

REFERENCE DATA
The bundled prepared receptor and ligand SDFs derive from PDB archive structures
1H1P, 1H1Q, 1H1R and 1H1S and their Chemical Component Dictionary connectivity.
PDB archive data are available under CC0 1.0 Universal Public Domain Dedication:
https://www.rcsb.org/pages/policies
https://www.wwpdb.org/about/usage-policies

Original structures:
https://www.rcsb.org/structure/1H1P
https://www.rcsb.org/structure/1H1Q
https://www.rcsb.org/structure/1H1R
https://www.rcsb.org/structure/1H1S
Associated structural-series publication: https://doi.org/10.1038/nsb842

The prepared structures are derived computational inputs, not unmodified deposited
structures. manifest.json records chain selection, ligand identity, preparation,
coordinate frame and file hashes. Computed hydrogens and water-removal choices
must not be mistaken for experimentally observed coordinates.

The versioned cdk2_1h1s_nu6094_v1 preparation transfers NU6094 heavy-atom
coordinates from 1H1Q into the ligand-free 1H1S receptor by protein backbone
alignment and selects a declared neutral 9H tautomer. It is a computational
template, not an observed NU6094–1H1S complex. The 1H1S receptor was chosen from
the known NU6102 complex, so validation using it is receptor-informed and
retrospective. NU6102 ligand coordinates are not included in this live
preparation. Its own manifest and template_alignment.json retain the source
identities, transformation and hashes; science_prepare.py reproduces these
derived files from the bundled prepared sources.

CDK2 validation fixtures and held-out coordinates

The file 1H1S_NU6102_observed.sdf contains deposited NU6102 heavy-atom
coordinates with Chemical Component Dictionary connectivity, in the original
1H1S coordinate frame. Source: PDB 1H1S, component 4SP.
https://www.rcsb.org/structure/1H1S
PDB archive data are available under CC0 1.0 Universal Public Domain Dedication:
https://www.wwpdb.org/about/usage-policies
Associated structural-series publication: https://doi.org/10.1038/nsb842

The coordinate file's SHA256 and provenance are included in each versioned
fixture. It is a held-out audit reference, read after inference completes;
it is not supplied to the molecular generation or placement workflow. The
receptor was selected from this known holo complex, so the validation remains
receptor-informed and retrospective, not a prospective docking benchmark.

The JSON files are immutable regression declarations and measured reference
records. Preserve their inputs, identities, hashes and exclusions. The
source_evidence and evidence fields name historical development records;
they are provenance labels, not required runtime file paths. The executable
comparison uses the embedded requests, controls, measurements and reference
hashes. A changed engine requires explicit review; the runner never updates
a baseline automatically. Counts across sampling seeds are not binding
probabilities, and docking scores are not measured affinity.

The neutral polar declaration records inputs before outcomes. Its unmeasured
reference values intentionally remain null. The optional OH-orientation
diagnostic examines saved structures without changing their coordinates,
selection or evidence. Its ligand-only MMFF energies are not binding energies
or protein-bound rotamer populations. Fixed hydrogen placement and the declared
neutral state do not establish populations at a particular pH.
